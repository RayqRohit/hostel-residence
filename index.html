Everyday essentials, all on campus

Arogya Card, included

The Arogya Card is part of your hostel fee, so care at Parul Sevashram Hospital on campus is covered from the day you move in: 10% off OPD medicines, 25% off OPD investigations and reports (CT scan, MRI and dental implants excluded), and cover of ₹1,00,000 for major surgery. The hospital runs 24x7 on a chargeable basis.

In the fee
  • Bed, study table and wardrobe
  • Mess meals, Wi-Fi. Laundry, six pairs a week.
  • Housekeeping. Water and power around the clock. A one time hostel kit.

Note: The hostel fee is not refundable, not transferable and cannot be paid pro rata. Rooms are allotted first come, first served.
