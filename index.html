FAQs

Questions students and parents ask

How much does a Parul University hostel cost per year?

Annual hostel fees for 2026-27 range from ₹79,000 to ₹3,50,000. A non AC bed in a shared room with a common washroom starts at ₹79,000. An AC single occupancy room in Ratan Tata Bhawan C, reserved for PG medical students, is the highest at ₹3,50,000. Most rooms fall between ₹1,00,000 and ₹1,50,000.

What is the difference between an attached and a common washroom?

Attached washrooms are en-suite within your room and shared only with your roommates. Common washrooms are shared among residents on the same floor and are cleaned multiple times daily by our housekeeping staff.

How much more does an AC room cost?

On average, upgrading from a non-AC to an AC room adds approximately ₹20,000 to ₹30,000 to your annual fee, depending on the occupancy level and hostel building.

Is laundry service included in the fee?

Yes, basic laundry service is included in the annual hostel fee for most standard accommodations.

Can I change my room later?

Room change requests are subject to availability and typically processed at the beginning of the next semester.
